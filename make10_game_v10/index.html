HOW TO PLAY

Make 10 from four digits

Use all 4 digits exactly once and build an expression equal to 10.

2 3 4 5 → (5 − 3) × 4 + 2 = 10
  • You can use + − × ÷ and parentheses.
  • Operators can be used more than once.
  • Tap or drag digits and operators.
  • You can reorder elements inside the expression.
  • You start with 2 hints and earn +1 after every 3 solved puzzles.
